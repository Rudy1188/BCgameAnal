Features

Everything you check, in one panel

No more scrolling through the history tab. BC Watchdog sits on the Crash page and updates the moment a round ends.

Trend board

The same trend balls you know from BC.Game: each streak fills a column, long streaks turn along the bottom row. Hover any ball to see its round ID and crash point.

Stats table

Pick any number of rounds and any payout point. See the chance, count and longest streak (Max Combo) on each side.

100,000 rounds of history

Scroll back through up to 100,000 rounds. Older rounds are worked out from the latest hash, so the history is complete.

Provably-fair check

Every crash point comes from BC.Game's published hash chain and salt, the same formula you can verify yourself.

Make it yours

Change rows, columns, cell and ball size, and every colour. Drag, resize or minimise the panel. It remembers where you left it.

Private by design

Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking, and nothing is sent to our servers or anyone else's.

Reading the board

Five colours, one glance

The board splits at 2×, like BC.Game. Big results get their own colour so they stand out. You can change any colour in settings.

Install

Set up in under a minute

Download the extension, load it into Chrome, and open the Crash page.

  1. Download and unzip

    bc-watchdog.zip, then unzip it to a folder you'll keep.

  2. Open extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).

  3. Load it

    Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Pin BC Watchdog to your toolbar.

  4. Play

    Open bc.game/game/crash. The panel appears on the right.

FAQ

Questions

Is BC Watchdog free?

Yes. It's free to download and use.

Can it predict the next round?

No. Each crash point comes from a hash that isn't known until the round ends, so past results don't predict the next one. BC Watchdog shows you what happened clearly; it doesn't tell you what will happen. Over 1,000,000 real rounds, every payout point returned about −1%, which is the house edge.

How does it get 100,000 rounds of history?

BC.Game's crash results form a hash chain: each round's hash is the SHA-256 of the next one. From the latest round's hash, the extension works backwards through every earlier round and calculates its crash point with the published salt.

Does it collect my data?

No. Settings and round history are stored in your browser only. The extension doesn't talk to any server except the BC.Game page you already have open. See the privacy policy.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome and other Chromium browsers such as Microsoft Edge, Brave and Opera.

Is it made by BC.Game?

No. BC Watchdog is an independent tool and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by BC.Game.

The panel doesn't show up. What do I do?

Reload the Crash tab after installing or updating the extension. Click the BC Watchdog icon in the toolbar to show or hide the panel. If it can't read a round, the panel says what it found on the page.

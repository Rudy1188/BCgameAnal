Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

BC Watchdog is a browser extension that shows a trend board and stats table on the BC.Game Crash page. This policy explains what data it handles. In short: everything stays in your browser.

What the extension reads

While you have a BC.Game page open, the extension reads Crash round results (round ID, hash and crash point) from that page so it can draw the trend board and stats. It does not read your account details, balance, bets, passwords or any other page.

What it stores

Round results and your panel settings (size, position, colours) are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your device. You can remove them at any time by removing the extension.

What it sends

Nothing. BC Watchdog has no account system, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • storage: to save round history and your settings on your device.
  • scripting and access to bc.game: to show the panel on the Crash page and read round results there. The extension runs on no other sites.

This website

bcwatchdog.com does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and to keep the site running.

Children

BC Watchdog is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

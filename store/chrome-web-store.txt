DESCRIPTION (copy everything between the lines)
------------------------------------------------------------
🐾 Your watchdog for BC.Game Crash. It never blinks.

BC.Game only lets you look back 2,000 rounds. BC Watchdog lets you look back 100,000.

It adds a live panel right inside the Crash page with the trend board you already know, a stats table you can bend any way you like, and a history 50 times deeper than the game shows you. Every round lands on the board the moment it ends.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔍 SEE PAST THE 2,000-ROUND LIMIT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Scroll back through up to 100,000 rounds. BC Watchdog takes the latest round's hash and walks back through BC.Game's provably-fair hash chain, so every older round and its crash point is recalculated, and nothing is missing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🟢 TREND BOARD, THE WAY YOU READ IT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Same style as BC.Game's own trend: each streak fills a column, and long streaks turn along the bottom row. Hover any ball to see its round ID and crash point.

🔴 1.00× instant crash
🟠 below 2×
🟢 2× and up
🟡 10× and up
⚪ 100× and up

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📊 STATS ON ANY RANGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Last 2,000 rounds, payout < 2"? Sure. Or the last 50,000 rounds at 3.5×. Pick any number of rounds from 10 to 100,000 and any payout point to see:
• Chance – how often each side hit
• Count – how many rounds
• Max Combo – the longest streak in a row

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Drag, resize, minimise or hide the panel. It remembers where you left it.
• Choose the rows, columns, cell size and ball size.
• Recolour every ball, cell and background.
• See the latest result on the toolbar badge.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Runs 100% in your browser
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• Sends no data to us or anyone else
• Runs only on bc.game

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🚀 HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Install BC Watchdog and pin it to your toolbar.
2. Open bc.game/game/crash.
3. The panel appears on the right. Click the icon to show or hide it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⚠️ GOOD TO KNOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BC Watchdog shows you what happened. It doesn't tell you what will happen. Crash results come from a hash that isn't known until the round ends, so past rounds can't predict the next one. Use it to understand the game, not to chase it.

BC Watchdog is an independent tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by BC.Game. For players 18+ only. Play responsibly.
